Photosynthesis is the process used by plants to convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose.
Chlorophyll is the green pigment in plant leaves that absorbs light energy for photosynthesis.
Water is absorbed by plant roots and transported through the xylem vessels to the leaves.
Carbon dioxide enters plant leaves through microscopic openings called stomata.
Oxygen is produced as a byproduct of the light-dependent reactions and released into the atmosphere.
